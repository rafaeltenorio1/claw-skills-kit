# inbox-triage: interview (Layer 2)

Asked once, when a client is onboarded. Each answer fills the client-config.md row named in "Fills".

| Id | Question | Suggested answer | Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Besides deadlines and payment problems, what should always count as urgent? | Anything with a deadline or a payment problem | extra urgent_rules |
| Q2 | Which kinds of email should stay out of the summary? | Promotions and newsletters stay out of the summary | sender_rules / suppression_rules |
| Q3 | Whose email must always be listed, whatever it says? | Our clients and our accountant | critical_senders |
