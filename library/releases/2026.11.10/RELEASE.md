# Release 2026.11.10

First release of the library for this host.

- **inbox-triage 0.1.0**: sorts new email into urgent, needs a reply and can wait, and posts one summary.
- **meeting-notes 0.1.0**: turns pasted meeting notes into a summary, decisions and action items.
- **team-digest 0.1.0**: posts an end-of-day digest to the team channel, once the client connects that channel.
